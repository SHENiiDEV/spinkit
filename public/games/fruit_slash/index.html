Side bets

Extra wagers on the next wave only

How side bets work
  1. Tick any side bet before you throw. Each one costs the stake shown on top (up to your main bet).
  2. Its odds depend on how wide your cut is, so they appear once you set the cut.
  3. It is settled on that one wave, win or lose, whatever happens to your main bet. A win is paid to your credit as stake × odds; it does not raise the main multiplier.
  4. Ticked side bets stay on for the next waves until you untick them.
